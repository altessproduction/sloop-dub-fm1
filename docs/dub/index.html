SLOOP — HOW TO PLAY

SLOOP is a four-track groovebox: three synth tracks and a drum track, a 64-step sequencer, songs and punch-in effects. The golden rule: hold a function button and the 16 white keys and four knobs change job — the screen always shows what they do. Tap the same button instead to open its pages; hold a layer and tap HOME to lock it open.

FIRST STEPS

  • Play the keys — the selected track's sound plays. ALGORITHM selects the track 1–4, PRESETS changes its sound, SELECT moves between pages.
  • PLAY starts the loop. REC while playing records on top (overdub), your timing preserved.
  • Hold REC ~2 s to clear the track; EDIT + OCT−/OCT+ is undo / redo.

RECORDING A FIRST LOOP

  • On an empty project, tap REC first: the REC screen appears.
  • free — no click: just play, and your playing sets the tempo and loop length.
  • tempo — record to the tempo you set; 1, 2 or 4 bars; optional one-bar count-in.

DRUMS — TRACK 4

16 drum sounds on the white keys, kick to cowbell; a black key doubles its left neighbour for fast rolls. Hold OCT−/OCT+ while playing for ghost and hard hits. 37 kits on the PRESETS knob — a sampled acoustic kit and 32 synthesized styles: 808, 909, trap, house, jungle, chiptune and more.

SOUNDS

Nine engines — analog, 4-op FM, phase distortion, trio, organ, voice, granular, lo-fi chip, sampler. 68 level-matched factory sounds browsed by kind, 32 user slots. One-key chords in SCL: TRIAD, 7TH, 9TH, SUS4, POWER. Envelope and LFO pages under ENV and LFO.

SONGS

Save up to four sections A–D (SAVE + keys 5–8), launch them live on the next bar (SAVE + keys 1–4), and record the arrangement as you play it (SAVE + key 14). In song mode, PLAY runs the whole chain.

IN THIS EMULATOR

  • Hold means click-and-hold or touch-and-hold a button — or hold its computer key (1–0 for the function buttons; full map in the line below).
  • Knobs turn by dragging vertically or scrolling; on a rotated phone, drag along the device's own up — toward its knobs.
  • Your work autosaves to this browser. The first save lands ~20 s after power-on at the earliest — the firmware's own pacing.
  • Not available here: the USB web editor, sample upload, USB audio and firmware install — and avoid MENU > HARDWARE CALIBRATION, which waits on the real key matrix.
